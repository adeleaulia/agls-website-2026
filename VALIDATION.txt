Confirmed code defects before the change
- The rendered video DOM had no autoplay attribute. The template supplied the
  lowercase autoplay prop to a React-backed runtime; it now uses autoPlay.
- A ref made a single play() attempt and silently discarded every rejection.
  There was no manual play/retry control or readiness/visibility recovery.
- Split mobile videos copied the upper video's paused state to the lower one.
  A blocked upper video could therefore stop the lower video too.
- playsinline was already present in the inspected DOM. Missing playsinline
  was NOT established as the original cause. The new code explicitly sets both
  inline attributes plus muted/defaultMuted before requesting playback.
- No actual iPhone or iOS Safari environment was available. These are confirmed
  implementation defects, not proof of the precise failure on the reporting
  device or its deployed host. Production URL/network traces were not supplied.
